# Link Analytics — Click Ledger

## Overview

Link Analytics is a read-only sample report for short links. Its job is to
make a small amount of illustrative data understandable while clearly labeling
the absence of a backend. The visual language borrows the discipline of a
printed ledger, not a SaaS admin template.

## Colors

- **Ledger paper** — `#EDE9DF`, the report ground.
- **Ink** — `#202B2A`, rules and main type.
- **Rust stamp** — `#BC573B`, primary metric and selected attention.
- **Archive blue** — `#476878`, chart bars and secondary metric.
- **Quiet green** — `#71866A`, active state.
- **Rule gray** — `#C4C6B9`, table separation.
- No gradients or unlabelled success colors.

## Typography

- Avenir Next / Trebuchet MS is used for report copy.
- Georgia creates the printed-report title and link names.
- Monospace carries range, metric labels, destinations, and values.

## Layout

- The report stamp and sample boundary arrive in the hero.
- Range controls precede metrics and alter the illustrative report values.
- A bar trace gives volume a visual rhythm before the link ledger.
- The table uses a short-link/destination split, click share, state, and an
  explicit open mark.
- Mobile moves the click share under each row and retains readable destinations.

## Elevation & Depth

- A selected link uses a flat paper shift and rust detail note.
- The report stamp has a small physical skew to suggest a printed artifact.
- No cards float above the page; rules do the grouping.

## Shapes

- Squared report sections and hairline rules.
- Range controls are the only compact pill-like controls.
- Bars are rectangular and deliberately sparse.

## Components

- **Report stamp** — sample data and no-backend boundary.
- **Window selector** — 24h, 7d, 30d sample ranges.
- **Metric strip** — clicks, links, click rate, top region.
- **Volume trace** — illustrative bar chart, not live telemetry.
- **Link ledger** — selectable row with destination and click share.
- **Detail note** — selected row explains the local demo behavior.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do label sample/estimated values near their context.
- Do keep the short-link table as the primary object.
- Do make the range switch visibly change the report.
- Don't claim real attribution, tracking, or persistence.
- Don't use a generic dark dashboard skin.
- Don't add fabricated data sources or backend health indicators.
